SAMPLE UNIVERSITY STUDENT HANDBOOK (fictional document for workshop use)

1. Attendance Policy
Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in every course to be eligible to appear for semester-end examinations. Students with attendance between 65% and 74% may apply for condonation by submitting a medical certificate and paying a condonation fee of Rs. 500 per course. Attendance below 65% leads to detention in that course.

2. Library Rules
The central library is open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and is closed on Sundays and public holidays. Each student may borrow up to 4 books for 14 days. A late fee of Rs. 5 per day is charged per book after the due date. Lost books must be replaced or paid for at the current market price. Silence must be maintained in the reading halls.

3. Assessment and Grading
Each course is assessed through internal assessment (40 marks) and a semester-end exam (60 marks). Internal assessment consists of two class tests, one assignment and one presentation. A student must score at least 40% in the semester-end exam and 40% overall to pass. Grades are awarded on a 10-point scale: O (10) for 90 and above, A+ (9) for 80-89, A (8) for 70-79, B (7) for 60-69, C (6) for 50-59, P (5) for 40-49, and F for below 40.

4. Examination Rules
Students must carry their ID card to every exam. Mobile phones and smartwatches are not allowed in the exam hall. Students arriving more than 30 minutes late will not be allowed to enter. Use of unfair means results in a zero for that paper and may lead to suspension. Re-evaluation requests must be submitted within 7 days of result declaration with a fee of Rs. 1000 per paper.

5. Scholarships
A merit scholarship giving a 25% tuition fee waiver is awarded to students who score a CGPA of 9.0 or above in the previous year. Need-based financial aid is available for students whose family annual income is below Rs. 3 lakh; applications open in July. Scholarship holders must maintain at least 85% attendance.

6. Hostel
Hostel check-in time is 9:00 PM for all residents. Visitors are allowed only in the visitor lounge between 4 PM and 6 PM on weekends. Wi-Fi is provided in all rooms. Residents must inform the warden in advance for overnight leave.

7. Placement Cell
The placement cell conducts aptitude and interview training from semester 5. Students need a minimum CGPA of 6.5 and no active backlogs to be eligible for campus recruitment drives. Resume workshops are held every August.
